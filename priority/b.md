## Executive priority verdicts

1. **PARTLY PUBLISHED, not verified as already published in its entirety.** The exact eigenvalue of \(K_{p,q}\) is **Nikiforov (2017), Proposition 38, journal p. 105**, DOI [10.2298/AADM1701081N](https://doi.org/10.2298/AADM1701081N). That article explicitly **poses** the general optimization as **Problem 30, p. 102** rather than solving it. The adjacency case \(\alpha=0\), including its balanced bipartite equality case, is due to **G. Constantine (1985), Linear Algebra Appl. 65, 171–178**; degree-based least-\(A_\alpha\) bounds by **S. Wang, D. Wong, F. Tian (2020), Theorem 1.3**, DOI [10.1016/j.laa.2019.12.039](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.laa.2019.12.039), provide pertinent ingredients, especially for even \(n\). **I did not find a prior explicit proof asserting the exact global formula and uniqueness for every odd \(n\) and all \(0<\alpha<1/2\)**. “Not found” does not certify novelty.
2. **NOT FOUND.** I did not locate the Athanor-style quantitative assertion \(d_{\rm edit}(G,K_{p,q})\le n\sqrt{(1-\alpha)n\epsilon/(1-2\alpha)}+L_*\epsilon\) (or an equivalent explicit edit-distance stability theorem for the least \(A_\alpha\) eigenvalue at \(0<\alpha<1/2\)) in the screened literature. Generic spectral stability methods are not publication of this statement.
3. **PARTLY PUBLISHED; adjacent \(\alpha=0\) runner-up has prior art, positive-\(\alpha\) all-\(n\) claim not found.** **G.-D. Yu and Y.-Z. Fan, “The least eigenvalue of graphs,” Journal of Mathematical Research with Applications 32(6) (2012), 659–665, MR3060328**, claims in its accessible abstract a characterization of the *second minimum* of the adjacency least eigenvalue among graphs of fixed order; its full article and DOI could **not** be retrieved, so its precise parity-by-parity theorem number/wording and uniqueness cannot be independently certified from full text here. Their follow-up **“The least eigenvalue of graphs whose complements are 2-vertex or 2-edge connected,” Operations Research Transactions 17(2) (2013), 81–88, introduction p. 82** expressly identifies the 2012 complements-connected minimizer as the complement of two almost-equal cliques joined by one edge, i.e. \(K_{p,q}-e\); this supports overlap for the odd adjacency candidate. This is **not a positive-\(\alpha\) theorem**. Bell–Cvetković–Rowlinson–Simić (2008) independently supply substantial fixed-order/fixed-size adjacency precedents. For the claimed \(A_\alpha\) second distinct minimum at all \(n\) and all real parameters, no prior proof was found; **the repository itself labels this an unproved conjecture**, not a theorem. Do not advertise a universal \(A_\alpha\) runner-up result as proved or as first.

## Scope and the three precise statements

Let \(p=\lfloor n/2\rfloor\), \(q=\lceil n/2\rceil\), \(c=1-2\alpha\). Claim 1 is \(F_n(\alpha)=(\alpha n-\sqrt{\alpha^2n^2+4cpq})/2\) as the unique minimum up to isomorphism \(K_{p,q}\), for **every** \(n\ge2\), not merely the least eigenvalue of \(K_{p,q}\). Claim 2 is quantitative proximity in **graph edge edits** to that unique minimizer. Claim 3 means the **second distinct value** over all unlabeled \(n\)-vertex graphs, not \(\lambda_{n-1}\) of a fixed graph; conjectural \(K_{n/2-1,n/2+1}\) for even \(n\) and \(K_{p,q}-e\) for odd \(n\), including \(\alpha=0\) analogues. Confusions between these three notions easily produce false priority matches.

Repository primary source inspected on main: [README](https://github.com/iwasborninbali/paper-aalpha-least-eigenvalue/blob/main/README.md); [certificates/problem30odd/REPORT.md](https://github.com/iwasborninbali/paper-aalpha-least-eigenvalue/blob/main/certificates/problem30odd/REPORT.md), including its Priority check; [certificates/p30stability/REPORT.md](https://github.com/iwasborninbali/paper-aalpha-least-eigenvalue/blob/main/certificates/p30stability/REPORT.md), including its Priority check. The directory [certificates/guide-checks](https://github.com/iwasborninbali/paper-aalpha-least-eigenvalue/tree/main/certificates/guide-checks) contains scripts/check logs, **no REPORT.md** at the time of this audit (direct request returned 404); hence there is no third priority section to quote. These repository reports were read **for the claims and existing bounded bibliographies only**, not as evidence that a novelty claim is true. Did not consult priority/a.md or the other auditor.

The repository states and proves a particular explicit stability bound for \(0\le\epsilon<-F_n\): with \(\beta=1-\alpha\), \(\mu_*=2c(n-1)/n\), \(C_*=1+\beta\sqrt{n-1}(n-1)/\mu_*\), \(L_*=n^2(n-1)^2[1/\alpha+(n-1)(1+2C_*)/\mu_*]\),
\[
d_{\mathrm{edit}}(G,K_{p,q})\le n\sqrt{\beta n\epsilon/c}+L_*\epsilon.
\]
For larger \(\epsilon\), it gives the trivial \(\binom n2\) bound. A ground-state transform/Doob identity and a nonnegative three-term deficit decomposition underpin the proof; these **general techniques** are pre-existing mathematical ideas, not by themselves a published graph edit-distance result with the displayed inequality. The runner-up certificate explicitly proves only \(n=3\) for all real \(\alpha\); for \(n=3,\ldots,7\) it supplies exact rational checks at \(\alpha=0.1,0.25,0.4,0.49\) and supplementary numerical checks. It **does not prove** claim 3 for all \(n\), and all-\(n\) priority should not be conflated with all-\(n\) validity.
