# Prior-art and evidence audit — revised 2026-11-15

**Athanor lab / Aleksei Kudriashov.** This is a dated, bounded literature and evidence record for *The minimum least eigenvalue of A_alpha(G) for 0 < alpha < 1/2*. It incorporates **two independent audits**, conducted on **2026-11-15** and saved separately as [`priority/a.md`](https://github.com/iwasborninbali/paper-aalpha-least-eigenvalue/blob/agent/priority-a-2026-11-15/priority/a.md) and [`priority/b.md`](https://github.com/iwasborninbali/paper-aalpha-least-eigenvalue/blob/agent/priority-b-2026-11-15/priority/b.md), alongside the author's previous search and the proof/certificate reports. The auditors worked independently; their access decisions sometimes differ. **A negative search is not a priority/novelty certificate.** Publication language: **“we did not find the odd-order statement published (two independent bounded audits and the author's search)”**. Do not say “first”.

## Claim-by-claim priority outcomes

| Claim | Publication/status conclusion and precise boundary |
|---|---|
| All-order minimum, unique balanced complete bipartite graph | **Partly published**: Nikiforov (2017), **Problem 30**, journal p.102 poses the problem; **Proposition 38**, journal p.105 (preprint Proposition 37) already prints the complete-bipartite eigenvalue. The sharp adjacency least-eigenvalue bound (Constantine 1985, recalled as **Lemma 2.5** in Liu–Das–Shu 2020) and the signless-Laplacian decomposition yield the *even* case and uniqueness. For *odd* n, that bound is not tight. **We did not find the odd-order statement published (two independent bounded audits and the author's search)**. The article supplies a complete proof, not a claim that its conclusion is newly discovered in the historical literature. |
| Quantitative edit-distance stability | The nonnegative ground-state identity and explicit bound are proved in `certificates/p30stability/REPORT.md` and in the article. Neither audit found the **same** stability estimate in checked sources. The method is related to familiar ground-state transformations. **Not found** means only within the checked material. |
| Second distinct minimum | **Known adjacency priority:** G.-D. Yu and Y.-Z. Fan, *The least eigenvalue of graphs*, J. Math. Res. Appl. **32(6)** (2012), **659–665**, **MR3060328**; its accessible abstract announces a characterization of the unique fixed-order **adjacency** second minimizer. **Full text, theorem number, precise parity cases and DOI were not verified** by either audit; no stronger attribution should be made. The all-order positive-alpha classification is unproved. The old conjecture “K_{p,q}-e for all odd n and all alpha” is **false**: exact integer certificates for n=5,7 at alpha=1/20, n=9 at alpha=1/40 identify **K_{p,q}+e inside the larger class** as runner-up. At the four previous rational points (1/10,1/4,2/5,49/100) n=5,7 instead have K_{p,q}-e. |

## Audit scope, search dates, and what was actually searched

- **Audit a, 2026-11-15:** read available Nikiforov journal text, Lin–Xue–Shu, Fan–Yu–Wang, da Silva et al. 2023, da Silva et al. 2026 preprint, selected publisher excerpts and accessible supporting articles. It recorded an OpenAlex snapshot of **304 citing works** for Nikiforov, with an adjacent parent-page badge showing **305** (index refresh or discrepancy), and screened all 304 *titles* over **13 pages**. It followed apparently relevant titles with abstract or theorem inspection when possible, **not 304 full-text readings**.
- **Audit b, 2026-11-15:** independently title-screened the same **304** OpenAlex records on **13 pages**, plus targeted abstract/theorem follow-ups including Guo–Zhang, Chen–Li–Shiu, Yu–Fan 2013, Special Matrices 2024 and RAIRO 2023. It also records search terms, arXiv full-text/indexed searches and failed access to the primary 2012 article.
- **Author's earlier searches, 2026-11-15:** the original `priority/REPORT.md`, `problem30odd/REPORT.md`, `p30stability/REPORT.md` (in [background Athanor repository](https://github.com/iwasborninbali/athanor-aalpha-spectral)), and the paper-repository copies document queries for “Nikiforov Problem 30 minimum least eigenvalue”, “least A_alpha eigenvalue balanced complete bipartite”, “smallest A_alpha eigenvalue minimum odd”, “least A_alpha eigenvalue stability edit distance”, “second smallest least eigenvalue graph”, “Constantine 1985”, Bell–Cvetković–Rowlinson–Simić, and Yu–Fan. The guide searched OpenAlex (work [W2963258333](https://openalex.org/works/W2963258333)), Semantic Scholar, DOI/publisher pages, arXiv and indexed general web sources; it read selected relevant abstract/full-text materials.
- **Index and access boundaries:** the OpenAlex citing list is **not the entire global citation network**; missing citation edges, unindexed articles and unpublished theses remain possible. Neither audit read every cited paper cover to cover, nor audited all Bell et al. errata. Google Scholar was searched through indexed results, **not exhaustively traversed**. Some publisher PDFs were paywalled; screenshot services failed/timed out even where parsed text was accessible. The primary Yu–Fan 2012 and Liu–Das–Sun–Shu 2020 full texts remain important specific omissions. **No claim of novelty follows from these searches.**

Full audit files with their own query logs and exact citation-list page links: [audit a](https://github.com/iwasborninbali/paper-aalpha-least-eigenvalue/blob/agent/priority-a-2026-11-15/priority/a.md) and [audit b](https://github.com/iwasborninbali/paper-aalpha-least-eigenvalue/blob/agent/priority-b-2026-11-15/priority/b.md).

## Evidence provenance and revision-1 correction

- **General mathematical proof:** `certificates/problem30odd/REPORT.md`; **quantitative stability and identity:** `certificates/p30stability/REPORT.md`; independent proof reading in `certificates/guide-checks/README.md` in background repository. These establish all-order analytic results for all real 0<alpha<1/2 and are not finite extrapolations.
- **Exact high-alpha runner-up classification:** `certificates/p30stability/atlas_exact.py` and `atlas_exact.log`, complete NetworkX atlas up to n=7 at **four** rational alpha values 1/10, 1/4, 2/5, 49/100; integer Bareiss determinants and Sylvester's criterion, 20 order/alpha cases with n=3–7. The positive-alpha runner-up is **not** established on an open interval by these isolated checks.
- **Exact low-alpha runner-up classification, new on 2026-11-15:** `certificates/guide-checks/runnerup_exact.py`, `runnerup-exact.log` (paper repo **main**): nauty `geng` v2.9.3 generates complete graph6 catalogues of **34, 1044, 274668** nonisomorphic graphs at n=5,7,9; at (n,alpha,t)=(5,1/20,-481/250),(7,1/20,-7261/2500),(9,1/40,-20359/5000) all except K_{p,q} and K_{p,q}+e in the **larger** part have an exact positive-definite integer shifted matrix. The article independently displays negative exact quotient determinants for those two exceptional graphs to establish strict placement below the rational threshold. This **refutes the old odd-case conjecture**.
- **Numerical (not proof):** nauty `geng` + NumPy `p30-second-alpha0.log` reports the same odd low-alpha candidate at alpha=0 for n=5,7,9 (and unbalanced bipartite for n=4,6,8); `p30-second-small-alpha.log` samples alpha=0.005 through .09 for n=5..9; `p30-second.log` samples alpha=.1,.25,.4,.49 through n=9. `p30-threshold.log` is a floating-point **two-candidate** bisection for odd n=5,7,...,25, showing alpha_5≈.07310372, alpha_7≈.06013314, alpha_9≈.04916085, alpha_25≈.01921649 and n alpha_n approaching .5 in the tested range. Neither uniqueness for every n nor alpha_n~1/(2n) is proved.
- **Other exact checks:** `certificates/problem30odd/exact_sweep.py` checks all 1024 labelled graphs of order five and 1044 atlas classes of order seven at 14 rational alphas; n=9 contains **8662 sampled labelled masks**, not a full n=9 exact minimum enumeration. `certificate_checker.py` isolates 42 exact radicals; identity checks in `p30stability/identity_check.py` are exact algebraic corroboration. Finite tests do not replace the all-n proofs.
